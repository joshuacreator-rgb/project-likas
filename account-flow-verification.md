# Account Flow Verification

Desktop and mobile previews confirm that the role-choice login remains readable and that registration, recovery, and invitation acceptance share the same calm, high-contrast account shell. On mobile, role cards stack into large tap targets, account links remain visible below the primary sign-in button, and form controls fit within the viewport without horizontal overflow.

The operations experience includes a persisted first-login onboarding overlay with role-specific guidance for administrators, center staff, responders, and citizens. The User & Role Management workspace now exposes invitation creation, recent invitation status, one-time invitation links, and audit entries. Invitation roles are limited to Center staff and Responder, and acceptance requires a 10-character password plus an unexpired token.
